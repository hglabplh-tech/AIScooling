## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

* Use welcoming and inclusive language
* Respect different viewpoints and experiences
* Give and receive constructive feedback respectfully
* Focus on what is best for the project and the community
* Show empathy toward other community members

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

* Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
* Offensive, insulting, or derogatory comments
* Personal attacks or trolling
* Publishing private information without permission
* Sexualized language or imagery
* Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable standards of behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate, harmful, threatening, or offensive.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in actions such as:

* A private warning
* A public warning
* Temporary restriction from project participation
* Permanent removal from the project community

The responsible maintainer will determine the appropriate action based on the severity and context of the violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and any other official communication channels related to the project.
